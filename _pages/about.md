I am an applied economist working on development and social issues. I use experimental methods, survey data and administrative datasets to study what women and under-represented groups' participation in decision-making, labour markets, financial services and innovation, and what can close those gaps.

I am currently an Economics Fellow in the [Innovation Economy Section](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/economics/innovation-economics-and-policy-design/index) of the Department for Economics and Data Analytics at the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO), where I lead research on women’s participation in innovation and develop statistical indicators to measure it.

I hold a PhD in Economics from the [Geneva Graduate Institute](https://www.graduateinstitute.ch/). In my doctoral research, I use an RCT to investigate how gender stereotypes about women’s skills shape intra-household dynamics, in rural Bangladesh.

Prior to joining WIPO, I worked at the [Centre for Finance and Development](https://www.graduateinstitute.ch/cfd), the World Trade Organization, the International Labor Organization, and the German Institute for Economic Research (DIW Berlin).
